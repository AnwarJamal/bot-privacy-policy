# Privacy Policy – Tournament Bot **Last Updated:** November 8, 2026 ## 1. Introduction This Privacy Policy explains how Tournament Bot collects, uses, and protects user information when you interact with the bot on Discord. ## 2. Information We Collect Tournament Bot may collect the following information: - Discord User ID and username. - In-game usernames provided during tournament registration. - Tournament registration details. - Payment proof screenshots for paid tournaments, if applicable. ## 3. How We Use Information The information is used to: - Register users for tournaments. - Manage tournament slots and participants. - Verify tournament payments when applicable. - Contact participants regarding tournament updates. ## 4. Data Storage Information may be stored temporarily or in a database to support tournament features. We do not intentionally sell or share personal information with third parties. ## 5. Data Deletion Users may request the deletion of their stored information by contacting the bot administrators. ## 6. Security We take reasonable steps to protect user information. However, no system can guarantee complete security. ## 7. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. ## 8. Contact For privacy-related questions, please contact the Tournament Bot administrators through our Discord server.
